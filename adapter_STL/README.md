# Adapter STL

Originally, mycobot arm does not have a gripper-like end-effector. 
This adapter STL is designed to connect a mycobot arm flange and gripper of SO-101 leader arm. 
Corresponding SO-101 gripper part STL is [Here (SO-101 github repo)](https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100/blob/main/STL/SO101/Individual/Wrist_Roll_SO101.stl)

You can 3D print this adapter and attach it to the mycobot arm, then you can use the gripper of SO-101 leader arm with mycobot.
We highly recommend to use the support material for 3D printing, as the adapter has a hollow structure.

## BOM

You need to prepare some screws to attach the adapter to mycobot arm and SO-101 gripper.

Name | Quantity | Note
--- | --- | ---
M3x12mm screw | 4 | To attach the adapter to SO-101 gripper wrist roll part
M3 nut | 4 | To attach the adapter to SO-101 gripper wrist roll part
M2.5x10mm screw | 4 | To attach the adapter to mycobot arm flange
--- | --- | --- 
STS3215 1/147 | 1 | For the gripper of SO-101 leader arm.
